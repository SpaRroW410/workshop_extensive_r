Region 1 · Pre-test

10 questions on the basics. Not graded; no feedback while you answer.

A baseline before Region 1. Answer from what you know (it is fine not to know yet) and rate your confidence. After Region 1, the post-test shows your gain.

wt <- c(60, 72, NA, 58)
mean(wt)

What does this return?

  • 0, because the missing value counts as zero
  • An error, because NA is not a number
  • 63.3, the mean of the three known values
  • NA, because one of the values is missing
f <- factor(c("Secondary", "Primary", "Graduate"))

Which level is the reference level by default?

  • None; factors have no reference level by default
  • Primary, since it is the lowest education level
  • Graduate, since levels are sorted alphabetically
  • Secondary, since it is the first value listed

You want to compare mean BMI between women and men. Which test is the usual starting point?

  • A paired t-test
  • A two-sample t-test
  • A chi-square test
  • A test of correlation

A comparison of two groups gives p = 0.45. What is the best conclusion?

  • The groups differ, but by less than 45% of the mean
  • There is a 45% chance that the groups really differ
  • Weak evidence of a difference; not proof of none
  • The two groups have been shown to be the same
